# Clinician Discovery Conversation Guide (Bilingual: English + Telugu in English Script)

> **Context**: For speaking with experienced clinicians who are relatives, elders, or family friends.  
> **Tone**: Respectful, polite (*andi / meeru*), curious, and conversational.  
> **Rule**: Keep it like a natural chat. No tech jargon or AI pitches. Let them talk about their day and headaches.

---

### Step-by-Step Conversational Flow

#### Step 1: The Icebreaker (Daily Reality)
* **English**: "How are things at the clinic/hospital lately—what does a typical day look like for you from start to finish?"
* **Telugu**: *"Hospital/clinic lo work ela undi andi ee madhya? Morning start ayinappati nunchi evening varaku mee typical day routine ela untundi?"*

---

#### Step 2: The Mental Load (Finding Friction)
* **English**: "Outside of actually examining and talking to patients, what part of the day drains the most mental energy?"
* **Telugu**: *"Patients ni chudadam kakunda, rojantha lo mee energy ni and time ni ekkuva drain chese panulu em untayi andi?"*

---

#### Step 3: Handling Point-of-Care Uncertainty
* **English**: "When you run into an uncommon case or need to quickly double-check a guideline or drug dose between patients, what’s your go-to move?"
* **Telugu**: *"OP lo unnapudu edaina tricky case vachina, leda drug dose / guideline ventane cross-check cheyali anipisthe... usually meeru em chestharu andi?"*

---

#### Step 4: The Real-Life Experience (Digging into the Pain)
* **English**: "Walk me through what you did the last time that happened—how easy was it to get a clear answer while the patient was waiting?"
* **Telugu**: *"Recently ala jariginappudu meeru ela check chesaro konchem cheppara? Patient mundu wait chesthunna time lo fast ga clear answer dorakadam entha kastam ga anipinchindi?"*

---

#### Step 5: Existing Tools & Workarounds
* **English**: "What apps or websites do you and your colleagues actually keep open on your phone or computer during the day?"
* **Telugu**: *"OP lo kani rounds lo kani, meeru and mee colleagues phone lo leda computer lo eppudu regular ga use chese apps leda websites em untayi andi?"*

---

#### Step 6: The Gap (Where Current Solutions Fail)
* **English**: "Where do those tools usually let you down or slow you down when you need something urgently?"
* **Telugu**: *"Urgent ga edaina specific info kavalannapudu ee tools lo ekkada stuck avutharu? Ekkada time waste ayinattu leda chiraku anipisthundi?"*

---

#### Step 7: The MVP Wish (Finding the Highest-Value Problem)
* **English**: "If someone could wave a magic wand and take just one repetitive headache off your plate during shifts, what would you want gone first?"
* **Telugu**: *"Mee daily shift routine lo unna oka pedda headache ni completely teeseseyocchu ante... eh problem solve aithe meeku pedda relief ga untundi andi?"*

---

### Quick Probing Follow-ups (To Keep the Flow Going)

* *"Andulo ekkuva chiraku/frustration vachina part enti andi?"*  
  *(What was the most frustrating part about that?)*
* *"Deenikosam daily entha time spend cheyalsi vasthundi?"*  
  *(How much time do you end up spending on this daily?)*
* *"Mari appudu aa situation ni ela manage chesaru?"*  
  *(So how did you manage that situation then?)*
